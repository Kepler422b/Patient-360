FDA GUIDANCE FOR TYPE 2 DIABETES MANAGEMENT

1. Introduction
This document provides guidance on the management of Type 2 diabetes mellitus. Regular monitoring of HbA1c is recommended.

2. Medication Adherence
Metformin is generally the first-line medication. Patients should be advised on potential gastrointestinal side effects.

3. Safety and Monitoring
Renal function should be assessed before initiating treatment and at least annually thereafter.
