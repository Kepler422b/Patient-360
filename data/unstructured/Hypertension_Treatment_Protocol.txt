CLINICAL PROTOCOL: HYPERTENSION TREATMENT

1. Overview
Essential hypertension requires a comprehensive approach including lifestyle modifications and pharmacotherapy.

2. First-line Agents
Thiazide diuretics, calcium channel blockers, and ACE inhibitors or ARBs are recommended first-line options.

3. Contraindications
ACE inhibitors and ARBs should not be used in pregnant patients or those with a history of angioedema.
